Refund Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Cancelling Your Subscription

You can cancel your EngKor Monthly Challenge subscription at any time. Cancelling stops future billing, and you'll keep access through the end of the period you already paid for. Cancelling does not automatically refund the current period.

Refunds

  • Within 3 days of your payment: contact us at engkorproject@gmail.com and we'll issue a full refund for that charge, no questions asked.
  • Within 7 days of your payment, if you haven't submitted any writing/shadowing and haven't received any feedback yet: we'll also issue a full refund. This covers cases where you signed up but genuinely never got to try the Service.
  • Otherwise: refunds are considered case by case, for example if there was a billing error or you were charged after already cancelling.

We don't offer partial refunds for unused days within a period you've already started.

How Refunds Are Processed

All payments are processed by Paddle, our payment provider and merchant of record. Approved refunds are issued by Paddle back to your original payment method and can take several business days to appear, depending on your bank or card provider.

Contact

To request a refund or ask about this policy: engkorproject@gmail.com
